## Overview:

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee attendance patterns, including Presence %, Work From Home (WFH) %, and Sick Leave (SL) %. It helps HR teams and managers track employee availability trends, identify patterns, and support decision-making on workforce planning.

## Problem Statement

Organizations need a clear way to monitor employee attendance and availability trends. Traditional spreadsheets make it difficult to:

* Identify employees with frequent absences.
* Track remote work vs office presence.
* Detect day-of-week attendance patterns.
* This dashboard solves that by providing a visual, interactive tool for HR managers and business leaders.

## Data Coverage:

Time Period: April 2022 – June 2022 (dynamic selection possible)
Granularity: Employee-level, Date-level, and Day-of-week-level analysis

## Tools and Technologies

* Power BI Desktop → Data modeling, DAX calculations, dashboard design
* Excel/CSV → Raw data storage

## Key Insights

Overall Presence %: 91.9%

WFH %: 9.9% (peaked at 23% in May)

Sick Leave %: 1.1% (spikes in late May and June)

Attendance dips were observed mid-week and Fridays.

Certain employees show higher WFH rates compared to peers.


## Dashboard Components:

### 1. KPI Cards (Top Row)

Presence % → 91.9%

WFH % → 9.9%

SL % → 1.1%
(These cards provide quick, at-a-glance overall statistics)


### 2. Employee Presence Breakdown

Horizontal bar chart by employee, showing split of Presence, WFH, and SL.

Helps identify individuals with higher WFH or sick leave compared to peers.


### 3. Trend Analysis by Date

Presence % by Date (line chart) → Shows daily attendance percentage trend.

WFH % by Date (line chart) → Tracks work-from-home trend over time.

SL % by Date (line chart) → Monitors fluctuations in sick leave percentage.


### 4. Day-wise Trend

Bar chart by weekdays (Mon–Fri).

Highlights which weekdays have higher/lower attendance, WFH, or sick leave rates.

### 5. Filters / Interactivity

Employee Name slicer – Focus on specific employees.

Date slicer – Filter by specific months or ranges.



## Results & Conclusion

The dashboard demonstrates how attendance insights can be centralized and simplified for HR decision-making. It enables:

* Employee-level performance monitoring.
* Detection of remote work and sick leave patterns.
* Clear visual trends for workforce planning.
